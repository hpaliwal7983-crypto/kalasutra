KalaSutra Karigar AI V7

Voice conversation reliability update:
- Added MediaRecorder fallback for browsers where SpeechRecognition is unavailable (including common iPad/Safari cases).
- Recorded voice is sent to /api/ai/transcribe and transcribed with OpenAI gpt-4o-mini-transcribe by default.
- Existing OpenAI chat + TTS remain in place.
- Added warm welcome overlay with centered Karigar AI avatar and a user-gesture Talk button for browsers that block autoplay.
- Conversation mode can automatically listen again after the AI replies.
- Existing V6 interface, language selector, Add Product guide, business modules, and other app features are preserved.

Required Render environment variable:
OPENAI_API_KEY
Optional:
OPENAI_MODEL (defaults to gpt-5.6-luna)
OPENAI_TRANSCRIBE_MODEL (defaults to gpt-4o-mini-transcribe)
OPENAI_TTS_MODEL (defaults to gpt-4o-mini-tts)
OPENAI_TTS_VOICE (defaults to coral)
